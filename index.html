Our story

Snacks that carry the island with them.

Crispy Ceylon began with one idea: the snacks we grew up with — roasted cashews from the eastern coast, cassava fried the way our aunties made it, rice crackers dusted with spice — deserved to travel further than the local market stall.

Every bag is made from natural ingredients, sourced from Sri Lankan farmers, and packed without shortcuts. Our mission is simple: deliver authentic, honest snacks while supporting the growers and communities behind every crunch.
